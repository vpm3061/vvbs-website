Business growth FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I start with one service?+

Yes. Start with a website, ads, content or automation requirement, then expand the scope when needed.

Is ad spend included in the retainer?+

Ad spend is separate unless explicitly included in your proposal. Tools, shoots, travel and applicable taxes are also clarified before work begins.

What is included in my plan?+

Your proposal defines platforms, deliverables, creative volume, revisions and reporting. We agree these details before onboarding.

How do payments and onboarding work?+

Business plans require 50% advance and 50% on the agreed monthly milestone. The start date follows scope approval, payment and receipt of required access and assets.

Campaign support FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can we discuss LeaderIQ before choosing a package?+

Yes. Request a demo to discuss available features, access and onboarding for your team.

Do packages include round-the-clock support?+

Support hours, staffing and escalation arrangements are agreed in your proposal. Round-the-clock coverage is not included by default.

How are content delivery times decided?+

We agree content volume, required source materials, approval responsibilities and turnaround times before launch.

What are the campaign payment terms?+

Campaign work requires 100% advance before launch. Your proposal defines the scope, timeline and any separately quoted costs.
